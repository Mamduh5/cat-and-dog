# Gameplay Art Direction Spec

## Project: Backyard Ballistics

## Visual reboot: character + world appearance

---

## 1) Core art direction

### Style

**Hand-drawn storybook**

### Visual feeling

* warm
* painterly
* charming
* slightly whimsical
* readable at gameplay distance
* expressive without becoming mascot-cartoon
* feels like an illustrated children’s book world

### Rendering style

* soft hand-drawn outlines
* visible brush-like texture
* painted shading, not flat vector fill
* slightly imperfect natural shapes
* soft lighting
* cozy suburban outdoor mood

### Must avoid

* photorealism
* anime style
* humanoid furry style
* hard-edged military realism
* glossy mobile-game plastic rendering
* looking too similar to the classic old cat-vs-dog throwing game

---

# 2) Character design rules

## Shared rules for both characters

* must look like **real animals first**
* default posture is **four-legged**
* no human-like standing combat pose
* no human hands
* no wearing clothes
* expressions are readable but still animal-like
* silhouette must read clearly from far away
* each character should have a different body language and motion feel

### Readability rule

At a glance, even without color:

* the left character must clearly read as **cat**
* the right character must clearly read as **corgi dog**

---

# 3) Character A — Thai Cat

## Identity

A **normal-proportion Thai cat**, elegant and agile.

## Inspiration

Thai / Siamese-type visual direction, but still natural and storybook-stylized.

## Body shape

* slim body
* long legs
* light frame
* elegant neck
* small-to-medium head
* narrow shoulders
* slightly arched flexible spine
* long tail with smooth curve
* compact paws

## Silhouette goals

The cat should instantly read as:

* light
* graceful
* precise
* alert

## Face shape

* triangular face
* refined muzzle
* sharper cheek line than the dog
* almond-shaped eyes
* tall pointed ears
* narrow nose bridge

## Eyes

Best choice:

* **clear blue eyes** or **soft amber-blue storybook blue**
* bright and alert
* not oversized cartoon eyes

## Fur / coat

Recommended main coat:

* **warm cream body**
* darker face mask
* darker ears
* darker paws
* darker tail tip

This gives strong Thai-cat identity and a beautiful storybook palette.

### Recommended color palette

* body: warm cream / pale beige
* ears + face mask + tail: soft seal brown
* inner ears: dusty pink
* nose: soft charcoal-brown or pink-brown
* eyes: blue

## Texture

* short sleek fur
* smooth brush texture
* subtle fur indication, not hyper-detailed strands

## Personality through appearance

* clever
* elegant
* focused
* slightly smug
* calm but dangerous when aiming

## Idle posture

* low balanced stance
* tail moves gently
* ears attentive
* body looks ready to move at any moment

## Action body language

When attacking:

* body lowers and braces
* neck extends slightly
* hind legs push firmly into ground
* tail straightens for balance
* the cat looks precise and controlled

---

# 4) Character B — Corgi Dog

## Identity

A **corgi**, sturdy, cute, determined, and a little ridiculous because it carries a launcher on its back.

## Body shape

* long torso
* short legs
* thick chest
* larger paws than the cat
* thicker neck
* compact heavy body mass
* large upright ears
* rounded rear

## Silhouette goals

The dog should instantly read as:

* sturdy
* lovable
* heavy
* energetic
* a little funny

## Face shape

* broader muzzle than cat
* rounded forehead
* larger nose
* visible cheeks
* expressive eyebrows optional through fur shape
* wide-set eyes

## Eyes

Best choice:

* warm brown
* friendly but determined
* slightly rounder than the cat’s eyes

## Fur / coat

Recommended main coat:

* **orange-red and white corgi coat**

### Recommended color palette

* upper body: warm orange / golden rust
* lower muzzle / chest / belly / paws: white or cream
* nose: dark brown or charcoal
* ears: orange outside, pinkish inner ear
* eyes: brown

## Texture

* soft medium fur
* slightly fluffier than cat
* painterly edges on chest and rear fur
* avoid too much realism

## Personality through appearance

* stubborn
* enthusiastic
* confident
* goofy but serious about battle
* looks proud of its ridiculous launcher

## Idle posture

* low sturdy stance
* head up
* ears alert
* tail area lively
* slight weight shift from launcher load

## Action body language

When attacking:

* body plants firmly
* legs spread slightly for balance
* neck tightens before firing
* whole body reacts to recoil
* looks forceful rather than elegant

---

# 5) Cat weapon appearance

## Weapon concept

A **small handcrafted mouth-held launcher**

The cat does **not** hold it like a human tool.

## Overall look

* compact
* clever
* handmade
* lightweight
* more precise than powerful

## Construction style

Use materials like:

* bamboo
* polished wood
* leather straps
* rope binding
* tiny metal fittings
* maybe a simple cloth wrap for bite grip

## Shape

Best direction:
**slingshot / tube-launcher hybrid**

Meaning:

* front end points forward
* center has a bite-bar / grip section for the mouth
* small side tension structure or compact barrel body
* elegant narrow profile

## How the cat uses it

* launcher held from the mouth by a bite grip
* forebody helps stabilize via posture
* hind legs brace the body
* no human-like “holding with hands”
* aim comes from body angle and head direction

## Visual personality

* nimble
* precise
* handcrafted
* smart
* quiet but deadly

## Color/material palette

* bamboo tan
* medium wood brown
* muted leather brown
* a few dark metal details
* maybe a subtle blue fabric accent to match the cat’s cool elegance

## Must avoid

* large bazooka look
* military rifle silhouette
* modern gun appearance
* human-style trigger grip

---

# 6) Dog weapon appearance

## Weapon concept

A **back-mounted backyard launcher** inspired by anti-air silhouette, but goofy and homemade.

Important:
This should feel like a **junk-built backyard machine**, not a real military weapon.

## Overall look

* oversized relative to the corgi
* funny but believable
* strapped to a harness on the corgi’s back
* sturdy and loud-looking
* more forceful and messy than the cat weapon

## Construction style

Use materials like:

* painted wood planks
* bolts
* copper pipes
* small tin plates
* springs
* patched metal parts
* old toolbox/scrap vibe
* optional tiny rotating bracket or elevation arm

## Shape

Best direction:

* compact launcher body sitting above the back
* slightly turret-like
* angled upward
* visible support frame
* not too tall
* stable enough to read clearly in side view

Think:
**“backpack artillery contraption built in a suburban shed.”**

## Harness

* padded chest strap
* belly strap
* side buckles
* slightly soft/friendly materials so it still feels storybook
* harness should visually integrate with corgi body without covering too much of the silhouette

## How the dog uses it

* weapon is mounted on back
* dog aims by body shift / stance / small mechanism adjustment
* firing causes full-body recoil
* the corgi looks like it is carrying a machine slightly too big for itself

## Visual personality

* sturdy
* noisy
* chaotic
* funny
* overbuilt
* proud

## Color/material palette

* warm wood brown
* muted red or faded orange painted panels
* dull brass/copper
* gray metal fittings
* leather straps
* maybe a tiny flag/tag/charm if you want extra personality

## Must avoid

* realistic tank turret
* dark tactical military look
* realistic war-machine detail
* futuristic weapon design

# 7) World / battlefield appearance

## Setting

**Illustrated suburban split-yard backyard**

## Layout identity

* cat yard on the left
* dog yard on the right
* central dividing fence/wall area
* both sides should feel like two neighboring homes

## Mood

* cozy
* warm daylight
* playful suburban rivalry
* storybook neighborhood

## Background style

* painted sky
* soft clouds
* warm sunlight
* layered bushes and grass
* flowers and small yard objects
* wooden fences
* a little environmental clutter for charm

## Cat-side flavor props

Examples:

* flower pots
* small stepping stones
* a tidy bush
* maybe a low tree branch
* a neat garden feel

## Dog-side flavor props

Examples:

* doghouse
* chew toy
* bucket
* a rougher patch of yard
* slightly messier playful energy

## Shared center

* fence / barrier / wall
* must visually fit the new storybook world
* should feel like a shared neighborhood divider, not a random game obstacle

## Palette

* warm greens
* light blue sky
* beige wood
* soft flower accents
* golden sunlight
* subtle earthy browns

## Must avoid

* empty plain field
* overly clean sterile background
* photorealistic backyard
* too many fine details that hurt gameplay readability

# 8) Motion personality by appearance

This is still appearance-focused, but Codex should understand the intended feel.

## Cat motion feel

* light
* quick
* accurate
* springy
* elegant
* controlled recoil

## Dog motion feel

* weighty
* sturdy
* chunky
* forceful
* bigger recoil
* slightly clumsy in a charming way

---

# 9) Do / Don’t list for Codex

## Do

* redesign the cat to clearly read as a Thai cat
* redesign the dog to clearly read as a corgi
* keep both primarily four-legged and animal-like
* use hand-drawn storybook rendering
* make the cat launcher small, clever, and mouth-held
* make the dog launcher back-mounted, homemade, and funny
* update the battlefield background to match the new world
* preserve gameplay readability above decorative detail

## Don’t

* do not make them humanoid
* do not make them stand upright like people
* do not make them look like the classic old cat-vs-dog game
* do not make the launcher designs realistic military weapons
* do not switch to hard vector/cartoon or photoreal style
* do not overload the screen with detail that obscures projectile action

---

# 10) Short final appearance summary

## Cat

A slim cream-and-seal Thai cat with blue eyes, pointed ears, and an elegant long tail. It uses a compact handmade mouth-held launcher made of bamboo, wood, leather, and rope. Its silhouette should feel agile, refined, and clever.

## Dog

A red-and-white corgi with a long body, short legs, large ears, and a sturdy, lovable silhouette. It carries a goofy homemade back-mounted launcher built from wood, metal scraps, springs, and straps. Its silhouette should feel sturdy, funny, and forceful.

## World

A warm illustrated suburban backyard split into cat side and dog side, with a central divider and cozy storybook scenery.
